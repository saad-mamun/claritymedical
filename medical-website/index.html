Ask our team →
How do I book an appointment?+

Use the appointment form above, call our care team, or ask your physician to send a referral. We will confirm your preferred time within one business hour.

How long does an MRI take?+

Most MRI appointments take between 30 and 60 minutes, depending on the area being scanned. We will explain everything before you begin.

Do I need to prepare before a CT scan?+

Preparation varies by scan. Some exams may require fasting or contrast preparation, and our team will provide clear instructions when you book.

When will I receive my report?+

Many routine imaging reports are available the same day. Your referring doctor will receive the final report securely.

Do you provide emergency diagnostic services?+

We support urgent referrals during opening hours. For a medical emergency, please contact your local emergency service immediately.
