MRI Scan

Detailed imaging
without radiation.

MRI uses a powerful magnetic field and radio waves to create highly detailed images of the inside of your body. It is particularly useful for looking at the brain, spine, joints, muscles, and soft tissue.

Our radiographers explain the process before you begin, stay in contact throughout, and make the room as calm as possible.

What an MRI can help with

  • Brain and neurological conditions
  • Spinal and joint pain
  • Muscle, tendon, and ligament injuries
  • Abdominal and pelvic conditions

Before your appointment

Wear comfortable clothing and leave metal objects at home. Tell us if you have an implant, pacemaker, or are pregnant. Most MRI scans need no special preparation.

During your scan

You will lie comfortably on a padded table that moves into the scanner. The technologist can see and hear you at all times, and you will receive hearing protection.

CT Scan

Fast answers
when they matter.

CT uses a series of X-ray images and computer processing to create detailed cross-sectional views of the body. It is quick, precise, and especially useful when your care team needs answers without delay.

Our low-dose CT technology is designed to balance excellent image quality with responsible radiation management.

What a CT scan can help with

  • Chest and lung conditions
  • Bone injuries and fractures
  • Abdominal and pelvic pain
  • Blood vessel and cardiac imaging

Before your appointment

Some CT scans require fasting or contrast. We will tell you exactly how to prepare when your appointment is confirmed. Please share any allergies or kidney conditions with our team.

During your scan

You will lie on a table that moves through the short, open scanner. The scan itself is painless and usually takes only a few minutes.

Before you visit

Questions,
made simple.

We will always explain your individual preparation when we confirm your appointment.

Will my scan be uncomfortable?+

Imaging should not be painful. MRI requires you to stay still, and CT is usually completed in just a few minutes. Let your technologist know if you feel uncomfortable.

How quickly will I receive my report?+

Your images are reviewed by a specialist radiologist. Many routine reports are available the same day, and your referring doctor receives the final report securely.

Can I bring someone with me?+

Yes. A companion can join you in the waiting area. For safety, they may need to wait outside the scan room during imaging.
